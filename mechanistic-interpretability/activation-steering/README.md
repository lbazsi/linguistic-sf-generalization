# Activation Steering

Estimate feature-minus-control activation directions from semantic-ID-matched linguistic pairs under each pretrained base model. Use training IDs only, with separate development material for exploratory manipulation checks. Save unit directions, randomized and label-shuffled alternatives, and independently estimated residual/MLP site-calibration scales; never retain token activations.

For all 14 features and each registered control and feature adapter, screen both `prompt_last` and `generation` intervention policies at every layer and residual/MLP site. The signed strength grid uses fractions `[-0.25,-0.10,-0.03,0,0.03,0.10,0.25]` multiplied by the square root of hidden width; the direction is scaled by a site-specific activation RMS (`relative_activation_norm_v2`). Measure the change in welfare-supporting-minus-opposing option log likelihood against an identical zero-strength baseline. Screening uses 20 scenarios; selection uses 80 separate validation scenarios to choose one nonzero configuration per feature–adapter pair, yielding 28 selections. Retain all 56 screening grids and evaluate selected random/shuffled controls.

Final evaluation uses 100 untouched scenarios for each selected configuration, with matched steered and unsteered open-ended continuations. Generate at least 48 and at most 512 tokens, with a sentence-boundary stopping rule, fixed seeds and generation batches of 32. The 5,600 responses are judged by two independent scoring calls in batches of 20, with coverage checks, paired estimates, cluster uncertainty and FDR correction. Inspect stratified continuations for coherence, truncation, extraneous sections and language switching; the token-limit gate does not replace this review.

Earlier exploratory pilots that consulted linguistic test examples cannot be characterized as held-out confirmation. A likelihood-proxy shift, even with successful intervention calibration, is not equivalent to demonstration of a grammatical change in text or a causal explanation of fine-tuning. See the [parent methodology](../README.md).
